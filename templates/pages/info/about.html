What is BIMS?

BIMS is a state-of-the-art Barangay Information Management System engineered to digitize local governance and modernize the issuance of official clearances and community records. It replaces antiquated manual logbooks with a hyper-fast, encrypted digital infrastructure — specifically optimized for Philippine Barangay operations.
