What is BIMS?

BIMS is a specialized Barangay Information Management System designed to digitize local governance and streamline the issuance of Barangay clearances, social certifications, and community records. It replaces manual logbooks with a fast, searchable, and secure digital system — purpose-built for Philippine Barangay operations.

{# ── Key Features ── #}

Key Features (Community Edition)

  • Unlimited Resident Profiling: Full bio-data with sectoral tagging (Senior Citizen, PWD, Solo Parent, 4Ps, Indigent).
  • Household Mapping: Group residents by household head and purok/sitio for census-style tracking.
  • Instant HTMX Search: Find any resident among thousands in milliseconds.
  • Social Certificate Printing: Indigency, Residency, and First-Time Jobseeker certifications.
  • Excel/CSV Export: Download the entire masterlist for backup or external reporting.
