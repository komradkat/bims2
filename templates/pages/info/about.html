About BIMS

Barangay Information Management System v2.0

Our Mission

BIMS is designed to empower local barangays in the Philippines by providing a modern, efficient, and transparent way to manage community data and services. Our goal is to digitize the manual processes of governance, making it easier for officials to serve their constituents.

Key Features

  • Resident Information: Securely store and manage resident records and demographics.
  • Automated Certificates: Generate clearances, permits, and certifications with professional templates.
  • Justice & Blotter: Track community cases, hearings, and resolutions.
  • Financial Tracking: Manage assessments, payments, and official receipts.
  • GIS Mapping: Visualize community layout and infrastructure with integrated spatial data.

Commitment to Security

Data privacy is our top priority. BIMS implements industry-standard encryption, role-based access control, and comprehensive audit logging to ensure that sensitive barangay information remains protected and used only for official purposes.
