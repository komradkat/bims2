Privacy Policy

Committed to Data Protection and Transparency

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Barangay Information Management System (BIMS) collects, uses, and protects personal information in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information necessary for official barangay administration and services, including:

  • Identity Data: Full name, date of birth, gender, and nationality.
  • Contact Data: Address, contact numbers, and email addresses.
  • Official Records: Civil status, occupation, and community-related documentation.

2. Use of Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Issuance of official certificates, clearances, and permits.
  • Community census and demographic analysis.
  • Public safety, disaster response, and justice administration (Blotter records).
  • Maintenance of accurate resident registries.

3. Data Security

BIMS employs physical, technical, and organizational security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. This includes encryption and strictly regulated role-based access for barangay staff.

4. Rights of the Data Subject

Residents have the right to request access to their personal data, correct inaccuracies, or inquire about the use of their information by contacting the Barangay Administration Office.
