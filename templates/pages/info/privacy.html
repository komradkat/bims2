Privacy Policy

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173)

{# ── Preamble ── #}
Philippine Data Privacy Act (DPA)
This policy governs how personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected within the Barangay Information Management System (BIMS).
{# ── Section 1: Data Controller vs. Processor ── #}

1. Data Controller vs. Data Processor

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the following roles are defined:

Role Entity Responsibility
Data Controller The Barangay Owns and is responsible for the personal data of its residents. The Barangay is responsible for obtaining proper consent.
Data Processor BIMS (The System) Processes and stores data on behalf of the Barangay as instructed. BIMS does not independently decide use.
{# ── Section 2: Types of Data Collected ── #}

2. Types of Data Collected

The system collects and processes the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity Data: Full name (First, Middle, Last), date of birth, age, sex, and nationality/citizenship.
  • Contact & Location Data: Residential address (Purok/Sitio, House Number), contact numbers.
  • Civil Data: Civil status, occupation, and religion.
  • Sectoral Classification: Tags identifying Senior Citizens, Persons with Disabilities (PWD), Solo Parents, 4Ps beneficiaries, and Indigents.
  • Household Data: Household head designation and family groupings.
  • Official Records: Certificate issuance history, transaction numbers, and blotter records.
{# ── Section 3: Data Storage & Encryption ── #}

3. Data Storage & Encryption

All personal data is stored in a PostgreSQL database with the following security measures:

  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data fields are encrypted within the database.
  • Password Hashing: User account passwords are never stored in plain text. They are salted and hashed using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2/Argon2).
  • Role-Based Access Control: Only authenticated and authorized users can access data according to their assigned role.
  • Audit Logging: System activity is tracked to ensure accountability.
{# ── Section 4: Local Storage Clause ── #}

4. Local Storage Clause

Important: Local Deployment Only

All data collected and processed by BIMS remains within the local network of the Barangay and is not uploaded to any third-party cloud service without explicit administrative configuration. The system runs on a local machine within the Barangay premises.

{# ── Section 5: Data Retention ── #}

5. Data Retention

The Barangay (as Data Controller) is responsible for managing the lifecycle of resident data. This includes the obligation to archive or delete records for residents who have:

  • Permanently moved out of the Barangay's jurisdiction.
  • Passed away (with appropriate documentation).
  • Formally requested the erasure of their personal data, as is their right under the DPA.

The System provides tools for authorized administrators to archive or remove records in compliance with these obligations.

{# ── Section 6: Rights of the Data Subject ── #}

6. Rights of the Data Subject

Under R.A. 10173, every resident whose data is stored in BIMS has the right to:

  • Right to Be Informed: Know what data is being collected and why.
  • Right to Access: Request a copy of their personal data stored in the system.
  • Right to Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of their personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of their data for specific purposes.

To exercise these rights, residents may contact the Barangay Administration Office directly.
